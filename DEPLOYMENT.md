### Integration notes

- **ABI:** `contracts/artifacts/contracts/MafiaMarket.sol/MafiaMarket.json` (regenerated by `npx hardhat compile`).
- Everything is **`matchId`-keyed**; `createMatch` takes a `CreateMatchParams` struct; `settle(matchId, moves, revealedRoles, salt, transcriptCID)` is **`onlyOwner`** (host). Index `MatchCreated / BetPlaced / BettingLocked / MatchSettled / Claimed / RefundModeEntered / Refunded` for live odds & lifecycle.
- Settlement is **trust-minimized** (assumes `teeSigner` is the genuine 0G-TEE key); see `handover.md` and the design spec under `docs/superpowers/specs/`.
